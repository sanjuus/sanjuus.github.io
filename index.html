Carbon Footprint Tracker

A predictive web application that estimates weekly carbon emissions based on personal lifestyle habits, helping users understand their environmental impact.

Uses a Lasso Regression model to predict weekly CO₂ emissions from factors such as transportation, electricity and water consumption, diet, and waste generation.

Provides personalized eco-tips and a motivational environmental quote through a simple, interactive interface.
